About Me

Charles K. Neimog is a composer, professor at State University of Paraná, and postdoctoral researcher at the University of São Paulo, where he earned his PhD in Music. He develops open-source tools including pd4web, OpenScofo, and py4pd, with a focus on mobile phones for music creation and education. His work connects computer music, composition, and philosophy of technology, particularly the thought of Bernard Stiegler.
